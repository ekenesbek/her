# Recording Consent Checklist

This app records conversations, so consent must be explicit and visible before recording starts.

Minimum product requirements:

- Show a pre-recording consent screen before the first recording.
- Keep a visible recording indicator while audio capture is active.
- Make stop recording one tap away.
- Store recordings locally by default.
- Give users delete controls for audio, transcript, and summary.
- Do not upload transcript or audio unless the user enables backend summarization.
- For team or enterprise use, document the jurisdiction-specific recording policy before release.
